From grocery day to opening

Sat, Oct 31Grocery day
Buy everything on the shopping list (pages 2–3) except fresh seafood. Bring the chest freezer home and let it stand upright at least 4 hours before plugging in (24 hours if it was laid on its side). Run it empty until it reads −18°C before loading.
Sun, Nov 1Prep & test day
Make and freeze the first batch of frozen lumpia and sisig packs. Cook one of every dish, weigh portions against the costing sheet, and take menu photos. Cook rice for tomorrow's chaofan and chill it.
Mon, Nov 2Price day
Set final prices using the costing sheet (page 4) and local competitor prices. Print the SOPs and post them at each station.
Soft launchYour pick
Open to family, friends and the Weekend Food Hub Facebook Page. Take orders by Messenger, phone and walk-in. Buy fresh shrimp and squid only on the morning of a service day.
Online orderingWhen the app is ready
Website with Facebook login, GCash checkout and delivery fees, plus the Android staff app with label printing. Until then, use the paper SOP forms and manual orders.
How the quantities were set: enough for the first 3 service days at about 12 cooked meals a day (18 chaofan + 18 silog in total), and 10 packs each of frozen lumpia and sisig. This is a cautious start before any marketing. Restock from your daily counts. Paluto dishes are not included. Adjust after your first week of real sales.
