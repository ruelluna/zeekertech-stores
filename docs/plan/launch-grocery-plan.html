Prep to do before opening (Nov 1)

  • Rice scoop: find a cup or scoop that holds 1 cup (about 160 g) of cooked rice. Every order gets 2 scoops.
  • Rice yield: 1 kg raw rice makes about 8 orders. Cook the day before for chaofan and chill it within 1 hour.
  • Protein portions: pre-weigh 60 g (chaofan) and 80 g (sisig, tapa) portions into containers so each order is grab-and-cook.
  • Siomai and longganisa: count 3 siomai or 2 longganisa per order into bags.
  • Containers: test-pack one chaofan and one silog in the 750–1,000 ml container to make sure the lid closes.
  • Frozen packs: weigh sisig at 200 g per bag and count 10 lumpia per bag before freezing. Label each with name, date and weight.
