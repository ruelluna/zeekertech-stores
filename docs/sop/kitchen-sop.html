Weekend Food Hub · SOP-01 Preparation

Start 10:00 AM · Done by 12:45 PM

Key temperatures

≤ 5°CChiller: meat, seafood, cooked rice
≤ −18°CFreezer: frozen lumpia, sisig packs
5–60°CDanger zone. Max 2 hours total
≥ 63°CShrimp, squid, whole pork/beef cuts
≥ 71°CGround meat, siomai, longganisa, sisig
≥ 74°CReheating any cooked food

Personal hygiene

  • Wash hands 20 seconds with soap. Repeat after raw meat or seafood, phone, money, trash or the toilet.
  • Clean apron, hairnet or cap on. No rings, watches or nail polish.
  • Anyone with fever, vomiting, diarrhea or an open wound on the hands does not cook today.

Station opening checks

  • Chiller reads ≤ 5°C and freezer ≤ −18°C. Write both on the temperature log.
  • Sanitize counters, cutting boards and knives. Red board for raw meat/seafood, green for vegetables, white for cooked food.
  • Gas: smell for leaks, check hose and regulator. Fire extinguisher in place.
  • Label printer on and paper loaded (once the staff app is ready). Until then, masking tape and marker for order labels.

Market run & daily availability board

  • Check shrimp and squid: clear eyes, firm flesh, sea smell. Buy only what today's chaofan needs.
  • Weigh what you bought. Note today's price per kg.
  • Mark shrimp and squid Available (with kg on hand), Out, or Unknown on the availability board: the whiteboard for now, the staff app once it is ready.
  • Count frozen lumpia and sisig packs and write the count on the board (or enter it in the app).
  • Tell whoever takes orders (Messenger, phone, walk-in) what is out today.
Set the availability board before 1:00 PM. Otherwise every Shrimp and Seafood Chaofan order waits for a manual check.

Mise en place

  • Check the thaw tray first: meat moved to the chiller last night should be soft. If it is still frozen, thaw it sealed under cold running water and cook it today. Never thaw on the counter or in standing water.
  • Frozen packs for sale (lumpia, sisig) stay in the freezer. Do not thaw them. They are sold frozen or cooked from frozen.
  • Rice for chaofan: use rice cooked the day before and chilled ≤ 5°C. Break up clumps. Never use rice left at room temperature overnight.
  • Garlic rice (silog): cook fresh rice. Fry minced garlic until golden and keep it in a covered container.
  • Proteins: pre-weigh 60 g (chaofan) and 80 g (sisig, tapa) portions, and bag 3 siomai or 2 longganisa per order. Keep in the chiller until service.
  • Seafood: devein shrimp, clean squid. Portion 60 g per order and keep on ice or in the chiller.
  • Aromatics & sauces: chop onion, spring onion, chili. Prepare chili garlic and vinegar dip. Label with name and time made.
  • Eggs: check for cracks. Keep cracked eggs aside for immediate use, never for runny yolks.

Labels on prep containers

Every container: item name · date and time prepared · use-by · initials. Prepped seafood is used the same day. Prepped meat within 2 days.
