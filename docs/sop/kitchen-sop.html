SOP-01 · Preparation

Start 10:00 AM · Done by 12:45 PM

Personal hygiene

  • Wash hands 20 seconds with soap. Repeat after raw meat or seafood, phone, money, trash or the toilet.
  • Clean apron, hairnet or cap on. No rings, watches or nail polish.
  • Anyone with fever, vomiting, diarrhea or an open wound on the hands does not cook today.

Station opening checks

  • Chiller reads ≤ 5°C and freezer ≤ −18°C. Write both on the temperature log.
  • Sanitize counters, cutting boards and knives. Red board for raw meat/seafood, green for vegetables, white for cooked food.
  • Gas: smell for leaks, check hose and regulator. Fire extinguisher in place.
  • Label printer on, paper loaded, test label printed from the staff app.

Market run & daily availability board

  • Check shrimp, crab and mixed seafood: clear eyes, firm flesh, sea smell, crabs alive and moving.
  • Weigh what you bought. Note today's price per kg.
  • In the staff app, set each ingredient to Available (with kg on hand and today's price), Out, or Unknown.
  • Count frozen lumpia and sisig packs and enter the stock in the app.
Set the availability board before 1:00 PM. Otherwise every crab and shrimp order waits for a manual check.

Mise en place

  • Rice for chaofan: use rice cooked the day before and chilled ≤ 5°C. Break up clumps. Never use rice left at room temperature overnight.
  • Garlic rice (silog): cook fresh rice. Fry minced garlic until golden and keep it in a covered container.
  • Proteins: portion pork, beef, siomai, tapa, longganisa and sisig into labeled containers. Keep in the chiller until service.
  • Seafood: devein shrimp, clean crabs and mixed seafood. Keep on ice or in the chiller. Live crabs stay cool and damp, not submerged in fresh water.
  • Aromatics & sauces: chop onion, spring onion, chili. Prepare Cajun butter and black pepper sauce. Label with name and time made.
  • Eggs: check for cracks. Keep cracked eggs aside for immediate use, never for runny yolks.
  • Thaw frozen items in the chiller only, never on the counter or in standing water.

Labels on prep containers

Every container: item name · date and time prepared · use-by · initials. Prepped seafood is used the same day. Prepped meat within 2 days.

SOP-02 · Cooking & service

1:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Taking an order

  • Cook only orders that show Queued on the kitchen tablet. Unpaid or unconfirmed orders don't appear there.
  • Cook in order of pickup or delivery time, not arrival. Watch the timer on each card.
  • Tap Preparing when you start, so the customer sees progress.
  • Read every modifier: egg style, spice level, extras, notes and allergies.

Paluto weighing

  • Weigh the crab or seafood before cooking. Write the actual weight in the order if it differs from what was ordered.
  • Final price = today's price per kg × actual weight. Staff confirm it in the app before the customer pays.

Hygiene during service

  • Separate tongs and boards for raw and cooked. Never put cooked food back on a raw tray.
  • Wipe the station with sanitizer between orders. Change the sanitizer bucket every 2 hours.
  • Prepped protein out of the chiller more than 2 hours total is thrown away.
  • Taste with a clean spoon each time. Never the cooking ladle.

Packing & labels

  • Pack hot food in clean, closed containers. Sauces and chili garlic in separate cups.
  • Stick the printed label on each item: order code, customer name, product, modifiers.
  • Check the order against its label before tapping Ready.
  • Delivery orders: add the delivery slip and hand to the rider with the order code read aloud.
  • Frozen packs sold as-is go into the bag last, kept apart from hot food.

Problems during service

  • Ran out of an ingredient: set it to Out on the availability board immediately.
  • Order will miss its time: tell the counter, who updates the order and contacts the customer.
  • Burns, cuts or gas smell: stop cooking, turn off gas, get first aid. Safety before orders.

SOP-03 · After service

9:00 PM · Done by 10:30 PM

Close orders

  • Every order on the tablet is Picked up, Delivered, or Cancelled with a reason. No order left open.
  • Pause ordering in the app if not already closed by store hours.

Leftovers & cooling

  • Throw away cooked food that sat out more than 2 hours, and all cooked seafood not sold.
  • Cool rice for tomorrow's chaofan fast: spread in shallow trays, chill within 1 hour, cover. Use within 24 hours.
  • Cooked food to keep: from 60°C to 21°C within 2 hours, then to 5°C within 4 more hours. Label with date and time.
  • Unused raw seafood: check smell and texture. If fine, freeze tonight and label; if in doubt, throw it out.

Kitchen cleaning

  • Wok, pans and utensils: scrape, wash in hot soapy water, rinse, sanitize, air dry.
  • Cutting boards: wash, sanitize, stand upright to dry.
  • Stove and hood: degrease the stove top. Wipe the hood filter weekly (write the date on the log).
  • Fryer oil: strain if clear. Replace if dark, foamy or smoking early. Record changes.
  • Counters, chiller handles and door knobs: wash and sanitize.
  • Floor: sweep, mop with detergent, then let dry.
  • Trash: seafood shells and food waste bagged tight and taken out tonight. Wash bins weekly.
  • Towels and aprons to laundry. Sponges replaced every few days.

Safety shut-down

  • Gas regulator OFF. Stove knobs OFF.
  • Fryer off and unplugged. Rice cookers unplugged.
  • Chiller ≤ 5°C and freezer ≤ −18°C at closing. Write both on the log.
  • Windows locked, lights off, door locked.

Inventory & reorder

  • Fill in the daily inventory count (next page).
  • Update frozen pack counts in the app so tomorrow's website stock is right.
  • Anything under its reorder level goes on tomorrow's market list.
  • Record waste: what was thrown away, how much, and why.
