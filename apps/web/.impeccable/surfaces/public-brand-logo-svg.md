# Brand identity: Weekend Food Hub logo system

Scope: the logo system (primary lockup, label lockup, square icon, one-colour mono) and its files under `public/brand/`, plus the favicon and the staff app icon. Mode: Persuade (identity). Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine).

Audience and job: local families, barkada, workers and tourists recognising the kitchen on the Facebook Page, the storefront header and the label on their order bag.

Constraints: orange stays the brand colour; the mark prints in black on thermal order labels at small sizes; room for sub-brands (Sweet Treats by Yang); must read as deliberately made, not AI-generated; no claims beyond PRODUCT.md.

Translations from the world: the world's radish coral is translated to the brand orange (PRODUCT.md commitment); jars and pickles are not depicted, because the menu has none; the label itself is the carrier and is functional, since every order already gets a printed label.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The brand is a hand-marked kitchen label: a brush-script name over stamped slab caps, the way a cook labels a jar, a frozen pack or tonight's order. It refuses the round food-stall badge with spoon, fork and ribbon.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft label #CDAE86 with notched corners and dotted panel rules; ink charcoal #2E2A26 for every letter; brand orange #EA580C as the one saturated field (icon ground, rules on light grounds); dill green #5C7F4A for the leaf sprigs only; cabbage ruby #B21E4B reserved for Sweet Treats by Yang's label band. Two voices only: connected brush script for names (outlined, with a drawn swash), stamped wide-tracked slab caps for facts. Small stamped caption row above panels.

STORY: A customer sees a label someone in a real kitchen marked by hand, recognises it on the Page avatar, the site header and the sticker on their bag, and reads where it comes from: Tapilon, Daanbantayan.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Primary lockup: script "Weekend" set large; the d's exit stroke hooks right and sweeps back under the whole word, tapering out beneath the W and running just clear of the stamped "FOOD HUB" below, which sits between two dill sprigs; origin line TAPILON · DAANBANTAYAN under a dotted rule. Label lockup: the same inside a notched kraft label with a middot-separated caption row and a category row. Icon: orange notched square with a charcoal script "W" (charcoal, because kraft on orange falls near 2:1 contrast); masked icons (avatar, iOS, Android) drop the notched outline because the platform supplies the mask, and no icon carries a swash. Mono: black only, no sprigs in the compact cut, thermal-safe.

FORM: Brine calendar (dealt challenger adopted by the user; catalog food-fermentation-culinary-theater-pickling-brine-calendar; not on my grounded list). seed: 6d3142dba5a8980a

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
